# Product Owner Dashboard — Main View Demo

## Slide 1 — Overview

This is the Product Owner Dashboard. It gives a high-level view of product adoption, user experience, response quality, and measurement readiness.

The purpose is to answer a small set of strategic questions: Are people using the product? Are they satisfied? Is the experience responsive and useful? And which signals still need stronger evidence?

---

## Slide 2 — Product Experience Pulse

This section is the executive pulse of the dashboard.

It brings together the most important single-metric indicators: session and query volume, user satisfaction, end-to-end latency, and workflow completion.

These are high-level indicators designed for quick reading, not deep diagnosis.

The main value is to provide immediate visibility into the experience before looking at supporting trends.

---

## Slide 3 — Adoption and Engagement

This chart shows adoption and engagement over time.

It compares sessions and queries across the selected period and provides supporting context, such as queries per session and average session duration.

It helps answer whether usage is growing, stable, or changing over time, without going into technical request-level detail.

---

## Slide 4 — Channel Mix

This section presents the distribution of demand across product channels.

It shows where users are accessing the experience and how adoption is concentrated across web, Teams, mobile, and API usage.

This is a simple but useful product view for understanding usage patterns and channel mix.

---

## Slide 5 — Experience and Response Quality

This section keeps user satisfaction and latency side by side as adjacent experience signals.

It does not claim causation; it simply shows that the product may be performing well in both areas, while also providing supporting quality indicators such as groundedness, relevance quality, and intent resolution.

Retrieval quality and task adherence add further context on whether responses are helping users complete their intended tasks.

---

## Slide 6 — Trust and Measurement Readiness

This bottom section summarizes trust and measurement readiness.

It highlights where the signal is mature, such as the MLflow safety score, and where the metric is still pending validation or definition.

This is important because the dashboard does not hide uncertainty. It makes measurement gaps explicit instead of treating missing data as a completed result.

---

## Closing Summary

The main view is designed to give the Product Owner a quick, honest snapshot of product health.

It answers the core questions of adoption, satisfaction, responsiveness, and quality, while clearly signaling where metric coverage still needs to be improved.

This keeps the dashboard useful for product decision-making without overstating what the current telemetry can prove.
